# MICAN SCHOOLS CONNECT - Delete Management Plan

## Purpose
Keep both Super Admin and School Admin dashboards organised while protecting important records.

## Required Features
1. Archive records.
2. Move deleted records to Trash.
3. Restore records from Trash.
4. Permanently delete records with confirmation.
5. Bulk archive and delete.
6. Search and filter archived or deleted records.
7. Maintain an activity log.

## Permissions
- Super Admin: Manage records across schools.
- School Admin: Manage records belonging to their own school.
- Teachers and Students: No access to admin deletion controls.

## Safety
- Protect important examination and financial records.
- Check related records before permanent deletion.
- Never permanently delete records without confirmation.
- Preserve existing working features.
